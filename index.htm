  3. 22 JUN 2026

    On Modeling Awareness & Perceived Maturity of Decisions

    When one to several parties are involved in a certain domain of interaction where each party can sample a decision from a given state-space, each decision made, either conditionally or in a mutually exclusive manner, influences both the outcome, objectively speaking, and how that outcome is perceived by each party, subjectively speaking.


    An *objective paradigm* of an outcome is the least common denominator of evaluation shared by all the parties, anything above that can be considered an element of the party's *subjective paradigm*, which can be used to characterize that party strictly within the confines of the domain of interaction.


    The best possible outcome for each party, interacting in a domain, only takes into account its own subjective paradigm.


    Such outcome for each party is affected by: - Independent occurrences of uncontrolled events - The non-conscious decisions made by each party involved - The conscious decisions that are based on a minimum amount self-awareness


    Self-awareness is the ability of each party to recognize: - The objective paradigm shared - The subjective characteristics of each party involved including oneself


    The level of maturity of each party can be self-evaluated by the probability of each decision successfully participating in the best possible subjective outcome. However, such level can be evaluated by other parties only via the objective outcome.


    Successful participation probability of each decision in the best possible outcome for an entire domain of interaction can recursively be evaluated via an intermediate outcome resulting from decisions in a narrower domain window, and so on.
